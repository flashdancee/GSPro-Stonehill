# Stonehill course releases

## 0.4.0-beta.1 - 2026-09-08

- Generalized Hole 1's refinement into deterministic per-hole turf, woodland and review-camera settings for all nine holes.
- Rounded all seven mapped water features, excavated submerged basins, blended banks and aligned GKD penalty boundaries with the Unity water export.
- Replaced triangle-centre collider removal with shoreline clipping; protected original tees and greens and bounded terrain replay to water basins/banks.
- Added repeat-refinement and full-source-rebuild regression commands, per-hole review images, metadata validation, installation hashes and paired rollback packages.
- Preserved existing routing and yardages. Holes 3–9 outlines and some steep banks remain provisional. Hole 6 upper pond and Hole 7 pond traces were adjusted to protect existing tee/green envelopes and require photo review.
- All holes await user GSPro tee-shot, hazard and putting acceptance; this is a review build.
